# Design Specifications

## Branding & Identity
- **School Name**: Ahlul Bait Public School
- **Location**: Tharushah
- **Subtitle/Affiliation**: A Project of Ashghrai Organization
- **Core Offerings**: Play Group to Class 8 (Boys & Girls), Quality Education, Islamic Values, Modern Learning Facilities, Safe & Supportive Environment.

## Color Scheme (Extracted from Poster & Logo)
- **Primary Base**: Navy Blue (`#0f3b73`)
- **Accent/Highlight**: Gold/Yellow (`#f7c815`)
- **Text & Backgrounds**: White and Slate/Blue-50 for high contrast readability.

## Inspiration
- **Reference UI**: University of Calgary Admissions page structure (`https://sapl.ucalgary.ca/future-students/admissions`). Clean, academic, authoritative, structured sections.
- **Reference Assets**:
  - Logo: Globe with book/pen and a ribbon. (Currently displayed as a rounded image with gold border to handle the blurred background).
  - Poster: Used for color extraction and tone.

## UI/UX Max & Layout Constraints
- **Landscape Over Portrait**: The website must prioritize a horizontal/landscape layout over long vertical portrait scrolling.
- **Horizontal Galleries**: Facility/campus showcases are laid out side-by-side using native CSS horizontal scrolling and snap-points.
- **Elegant Scrollbars**: No hidden scrollbars on desktop; custom styled scrollbars provide clear horizontal scrolling affordances.
- **Uniform Idea**: Imagery should feature students in Navy Blue and Yellow uniforms, reflecting a modern but values-driven Islamic educational environment.
