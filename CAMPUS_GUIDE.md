# Campus Guide

This document outlines the 8 campuses included in our school network and how they are structured in the UI.

## Campuses
1. **North Campus**: Focused on Science & Technology.
2. **South Campus**: Renowned for Arts & Humanities.
3. **East Campus**: Sports and Physical Education center.
4. **West Campus**: Business and Entrepreneurship hub.
5. **Central Campus**: The historic main campus.
6. **Lakeside Campus**: Environmental and Ecological studies.
7. **Hilltop Campus**: Advanced Research facilities.
8. **Downtown Campus**: Urban studies and Media.

## Implementation Details
Each campus is rendered using the `CampusShowcase` component. This component accepts campus metadata (name, description, image placeholder, etc.) and uses Framer Motion variants to trigger an entrance animation when the user scrolls it into view.
